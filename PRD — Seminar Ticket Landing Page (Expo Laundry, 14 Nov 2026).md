# PRD — Seminar Ticket Landing Page (Expo Laundry, 14 Nov 2026)

Nov 2, 2026 · @Laundry Books ID

## Summary

A one-page, mobile-first sales page that turns paid-ad traffic into paid seats for Nirwan's seminar at Expo Laundry, ICE BSD City, on Saturday 14 Nov 2026. It goes live by **Thu 5 Nov**, which leaves 9 days of ad spend before the event.

Every buyer first leaves their name and WhatsApp number, then pays on Lynk.id. That gives the BERANI/LaundryBiz team a lead list even when someone doesn't finish paying. Post-event, the main goal is leads for BERANI and LaundryBiz services.

**Success metrics**

- Primary: paid tickets via the page (offline quota: 30 seats).
- Secondary: leads captured (name + WA), lead → paid rate, and cost per paid ticket from ads.
- Health: page loads in under 3 s on a mid-range Android over 4G; form completion ≥ 40% of people who tap a ticket button (proposed target).

## Audience and traffic

The page speaks to two buyers at once: current laundry owners feeling the competition, and prospective owners who want to start right. Most visitors arrive cold from paid ads on a phone, so the first screen must answer "what, when, where, how much" without scrolling.

**Traffic sources:** Meta ads (direct response), organic social from BERANI/LaundryBiz/LaundryBooks, WhatsApp shares and HIPLI groups. HIPLI members get their own Lynk link inside HIPLI groups and are not the page's main target.

**Objections the page must answer**

- "Is this just a franchise pitch?" The seminar teaches management. BERANI's core is laundry management, not franchise sales.
- "Who is the speaker, and why listen to him?" Nirwan runs BERANI, which manages 120+ outlets in Jabodetabek, Bandung, Semarang and Yogyakarta.
- "Is Rp350.000 worth it?" Anchor against Rp450.000, and list lunch, coffee break, certificate and souvenir.
- "I can't get to BSD." Point to the online option, if it's confirmed (see Open questions).

## Visual guidelines

The page should feel like the poster opened up into a page. Someone who clicked an ad showing the poster should recognize it instantly. The colour values below are approximate and taken from the image. Confirm them against the designer's source file.

| Element | Poster cue | Page rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Very light sky-blue wash with soft white glows | Light blue gradient background (≈ #EAF4FF → #FFFFFF); white cards on top |
| Primary colour | Bright blue for pills, price box and icons | One primary blue (≈ #1A8CFF) for CTAs, icons and the price box; no second accent colour |
| Text | Dark navy for facts, white on blue | Navy (≈ #1B2A4A) for body and facts; white only on blue fills |
| Headline | Heavy, all-caps sans in blue with a white outline | Extra-bold geometric sans, all caps, blue, for the hero title only; sentence case elsewhere |
| Shapes | Rounded pills, rounded cards, circular icon badges | Radius 16–24 px for cards, full pill for CTAs and chips; circular blue badges for date, venue and ticket icons |
| Speaker | Cut-out photo on a blue gradient card, name in bold caps | Reuse the same treatment for the speaker section and the hero |
| Benefits | Blue check-circle list | Same check icons |
| Logos | Expo Laundry at left; LaundryBiz Aggregator and BERANI at right | Same order in the top bar and footer |
| Red | Only in the "EXPO" wordmark | Never used in the UI |

**Fonts:** Poppins (or Plus Jakarta Sans) ExtraBold for the hero title, and the same family Regular/SemiBold for body text, loaded from Google Fonts.

**Mobile first:** design at 375 px wide. The hero fits title, date, venue, price and CTA above the fold, and the photo sits below the CTA on mobile.

## Leads form and follow-up

The form opens as a bottom sheet from any ticket button. It asks for four things, takes under 30 seconds, and saves before the visitor is sent to Lynk.id.

| Field | Type | Required | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nama lengkap | Text | Yes | Follow-up and certificate name |
| No. WhatsApp | Phone (accepts 08… or 62…, stored as 62…) | Yes | Follow-up channel |
| Status | Two tap buttons: "Sudah punya laundry" / "Mau buka laundry" | Yes | Routes the lead to Laundry Management or LaundryBiz afterwards |
| Kota | Text | No | Ties leads to the outlet network |
| Ticket chosen | Set by the button tapped | Auto | Shows which Lynk link they were sent to |
| Source | UTM tags from the ad URL | Auto | Measures which ad sold the ticket |

**Storage:** leads are saved in the page's built-in database, which the team can view and export as CSV. Google Sheets is the fallback if you'd rather work there.

**After submit:** a confirmation screen with "Lanjut ke Pembayaran" opens the right Lynk.id link in the same tab. A "Tanya via WhatsApp" link to 0811-2005-5578 comes with a pre-filled message.

**Follow-up rule:** each day the team compares leads against Lynk.id sales. Anyone who left their details but didn't pay gets one WhatsApp follow-up within 24 hours.

**Consent:** one line under the button, "Data Anda hanya dipakai tim BERANI/LaundryBiz untuk informasi seminar dan layanan terkait."

## Ad tracking

Without pixel access, UTM tags are the minimum. Each ad links to the page with utm\_source, utm\_campaign and utm\_content, and the form saves them with every lead.

- Meta Pixel events: PageView on load, InitiateCheckout on the ticket button, Lead on form submit. This needs the Pixel ID from whoever runs the ads. Limitation: the Meta Pixel script can't load on a page hosted here. If the ads need pixel optimization, the same HTML file moves to your own domain, such as a subdomain of berani-laundry.com. Until then, UTM tags carry the tracking.
- The Purchase event happens on Lynk.id, not on this page. Count paid tickets from Lynk.id sales and match them to leads by WhatsApp number.
- Report daily until the event: leads, paid tickets and ad spend, which gives cost per lead and cost per paid ticket.

## Scope

Version 1 sells tickets for one event. Anything that isn't needed to sell those seats by 14 Nov waits.

**In scope**

- One self-contained HTML page (Indonesian), mobile-first, published here with a private shareable link
- Leads form, lead storage with CSV export, redirect to Lynk.id
- WhatsApp hotline buttons, Google Maps link, FAQ
- UTM capture; a Pixel-ready version if the page moves to your domain

**Out of scope**

- Payment on the page itself (Lynk.id handles payment)
- Live seat counter tied to Lynk.id sales
- Accounts, logins, ticket QR codes, check-in
- A reusable multi-event seminar system (revisit after 14 Nov)
- An English version

## Open questions (block launch)

The first four change what the ticket section says, so they need answers before the build.

- [ ] **Online and ebook tickets:** the earlier plan was hybrid (offline, online, ebook bundle), but the poster shows only an in-person event. Does the page sell offline tickets only, or all three? If all three, what are the online and bundle prices?
- [ ] **"First 10 via BERANI" price:** what is the special price, and does it have its own Lynk.id link? Once 10 are sold, does the page switch to Rp350.000 automatically, or does the team update it by hand?
- [ ] **Member price on the page:** the poster shows Rp200.000 publicly, but the member link is meant only for HIPLI groups. Option A: show the price and send members to WhatsApp to get the link. Option B: leave the member price off the page.
- [ ] **Lynk.id links:** one URL per ticket type.
- [ ] **4 pillar copy:** the pillar names are set; Claude drafts one benefit line per pillar and you approve them.
- [ ] **Seats:** is the offline quota still 30? Does the ticket include Expo Laundry entry?
- [ ] **Refund and reschedule policy**, for the FAQ.
- [ ] **Assets:** high-res logos (Expo Laundry, LaundryBiz Aggregator, BERANI) and the speaker cut-out photo.
- [ ] **Hosting:** stay on the page here (no Pixel), or move to your own domain (Pixel works)? Is there a Pixel ID?
